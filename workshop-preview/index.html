Before you start

  1. A ChatGPT account ($20 Plus). You can use any LLM for your agent, but ChatGPT lets you use subscription tokens for it, which is handy if you don't want to accidentally run up a bill.
  2. On a PC? Everything works, you'll just use chat.filament.dm instead of the desktop app, and paste into the terminal with Shift+Insert instead of Ctrl+V. PC notes are flagged where they matter.
