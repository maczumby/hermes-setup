# Screenshot shot list — workshop guide

10 shots. The rule for what made the list: a screenshot earns its place when
the reader faces a choice (which option, which seat), has to find a buried
control, or needs to recognize what success looks like. Pages a link lands
you on, and outcomes people produce live in the exercise, don't get one.
(Cut on that rule: the sign-in page, the fresh backchannel, the
agent-answering-a-stranger shot.)

Save each as the exact filename into `hermes-setup/workshop-preview/img/`
while we're reviewing the preview (it moves to `workshop/img/` on promotion). PNG,
full window is fine (the guide scales them). Blur anything with a token.
Partial batches are fine — the pages show placeholders until each file
exists, and I push them as they land.
