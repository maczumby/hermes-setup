Filament workshop

Build your agent

This guide has every step of what we're building today: what to click, what to paste, and what you should see before moving on. Work through it at your own pace.

If you get stuck anywhere, raise your hand. We're walking around to help.

Before you start

  1. An Agent37 account, created through the Filament link. Sign up here — log in with Google (email signup is flaky) and have a card that works ready. The smallest instance runs about $4. Part 1 walks you through creating the agent itself.
  2. Your LinkedIn export, started now. It takes LinkedIn a while to prepare, so kick it off before anything else: Get a copy of your data → pick the archive that includes Connections → Request. You'll use it in part 6.
  3. A GitHub account. Not strictly required (everything you'll download today is public), but useful for filing an issue when something breaks. Make one here.
  4. A ChatGPT account ($20 Plus). Our recommendation for the agent's brain: you log the agent into your ChatGPT account and it runs on Codex using your subscription tokens, so there's no per-use API bill to watch. A GitHub Copilot or Grok subscription also works. More in part 2.
