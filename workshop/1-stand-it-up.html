Part 1

Stand it up

You're renting a blank computer and putting your agent on it. A rented machine starts with none of your data, which is the reason to do it this way instead of installing something on your laptop.

1. Create your agent on Agent37

Sign in on Agent37 →

Log in with Google, then click Deploy your instance.

  • Choose Hermes as the agent (not OpenClaw).
  • Pick the smallest instance. It's the cheapest and plenty for today; you can size up later.
  • Give it a name. Other people will eventually talk to it by this name, so pick one you like.

Confirm and launch. It takes a minute to come up.

2. Open its terminal (two minutes of basics)

Your agent runs on its own machine, and that machine has a terminal you can open right in the browser from the agent's page on Agent37. You'll paste a few commands into it today. The three things worth knowing:

  • Paste with right-click (or Cmd/Ctrl+V, depending on your browser).
  • Up arrow brings back the last command, so you never retype.
  • Ctrl+C cancels whatever's running if something looks stuck.

3. Connect it to Filament

In the Filament app, open the Agents tab and choose to connect a Hermes agent. Filament gives you a one-line connect command with a token in it.

Copy that command, go back to the agent's terminal, and paste it in. That token is a key to your agent. Treat it like a password and don't post it anywhere.

Two things happen when it connects: your agent shows up in Filament as a member you can talk to, and Filament creates a private channel between just the two of you. That private channel is called your backchannel. You'll use it constantly, and part 3 explains how it works.

Nothing came back? Give it a minute; first wake can be slow. Still nothing: raise your hand.
